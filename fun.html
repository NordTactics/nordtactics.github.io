NORDTACTICS on YouTube

The tactics. All the tactics...

Tactical video games are the bread and butter of my friend group. There’s nothing quite like being able to experience a challenging mission alongside your best friends, all while employing tactics and teamwork in a realistic sandbox to get the job done. It’s uber satisfying.

The NordTactics Youtube channel is all about tactical and milsim games, from my own team’s S.O.G. Prairie Fire Arma 3 to Ghost Recon, to Tactical Assault VR, we’re always up to some sort of virtual warfighting, usually thinking we’re way cooler than we really are.

The most incredible experiences on this channel are those that my fellow Team Savage members and I get to share with actual MACV-SOG Veterans of the Vietnam War. That’s right, we play with people who did the real thing. I am humbled and proud to call so many on this channel close friends.

NORDAURORA on YouTube

Gaming just like the good old days!

Since a young age I remember always wanting to share games with others. I can remember little Sam at eight years old being so excited when any family member would come down in the basement to watch me play games on my Playstation 2. The NordAurora YouTube channel Is all about exploring games that are less tactical and more adventurous. This is a smaller, more intimate experience that features mostly livestreamed content.

Join me on a journey to experience games like Red Dead Redemption 2, Mass Effect, Star Citizen, Ratchet & Clank, and many more.

TASK FORCE TIMBERWOLF

A community unlike any other.

Task Force Timberwolf, or TFTW, is a small, tight knit Arma 3 community that has been playing cooperative missions together for nearly seven years. Our goal is to provide immersive, enjoyable missions in a friendly and mature community setting. We buy heavily into Dslyecxi’s “Serious Fun” mindset, where teamwork, attitude, and the shared experience matter more than chasing special roles or personal spotlight.

TFTW plays regular weekend events that are friendly to most time zones in North America and Europe. We are always looking for people who are in it for the long haul, enjoy contributing to the team, and want to be part of a welcoming community of friends. Prospective members should be 18 or older and can apply through our website. Thanks for taking the time to learn about us, and we hope to see you out there.
