I am a veteran and media professional who loves connecting people. We have a lot more in common than we might think!

During nearly five years in the Army - jumping out of airplanes, training alongside NATO allies, learning from some incredible people, traveling the world, and making some of the closest friends of my life - I grew as a person. Still, I realized the rigid structure of the military left me unable to express my creativity in the ways I would like.

Becoming a civilian again left me seeking a sense of purpose to fill the void of life as a soldier. I discovered my calling in art and history. Joining Savage Game Design helped me discover a fascination for history and a deep respect for Vietnam War veterans. I have had the opportunity to develop passions for history, voice acting, graphic design, video production, and a few other ... areas.

Our team built a historical authenticity-focused video game about Special Operations in the Vietnam War called S.O.G. Prairie Fire; I love playing just as much as working behind the scenes! I feel a sense of urgency for the work we do; Vietnam veterans become fewer each year, so preserving their stories couldn't be more important.

I have discovered media's power to connect people, whether it be to history, the world around us, or to one another. This is what it means to be an artist to me. My goal is to approach all historical projects with respect, open-mindedness, and passion for doing these stories justice through authentic storytelling. I make it my mission to approach my work with an open mind and a sense of curiosity and fun.
